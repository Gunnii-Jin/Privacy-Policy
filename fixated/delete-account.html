Delete your account — Fixated

Fixated, published by Kerri Williams

The short version Delete your account in the app from Profile → Delete Account, or email tmrw.pod@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Your account and its data are deleted permanently.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Fixated and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately, and you're signed out. Want a copy first? Tap Export My Data on the same screen before deleting.

Delete it without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email tmrw.pod@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Fixated account. Send it from the email address on your account (if you used Sign in with Apple with a hidden email, tell us your Fixated display name and roughly when you signed up). We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, password, and your Google or Apple sign-in link.
  • Your profile: display name, profile picture, reader type, and age confirmation.
  • Your reading data: library, ratings, swipes, trope preferences, follows, and reading activity.
  • Everything you posted: comments, room posts, replies, prompt answers, reactions, and votes, including any images you attached.
  • Reports you filed, your block list, compatibility matches, and your notification tokens.

Deletion is permanent. There's no grace period and your account can't be recovered.

What stays

  • Title and trope suggestions you submitted stay in the catalog, but your account is no longer attached to them.
  • Anonymous “Where to read” totals were never linked to you, so they don't change.
  • Creator program waitlist sign-ups aren't linked to your Fixated account. To remove one, email tmrw.pod@gmail.com.
  • Purchase records for supporter subscriptions are kept by Google Play or the App Store, and by RevenueCat (an anonymous subscriber ID), as their payment and tax rules require.
  • Copies in our database backups are overwritten automatically within 30 days.
  • Subscriptions aren't cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel Support Fixated in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings first, so you aren't charged again.

Delete some data but keep your account

You can delete any comment, post, or reply you made, and remove titles from your library, at any time in the app. For anything else, email tmrw.pod@gmail.com and tell us what you'd like removed.

More detail is in the Fixated Privacy Policy.
