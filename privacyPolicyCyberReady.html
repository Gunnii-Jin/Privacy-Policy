Privacy Policy — CyberReady

Last updated: August 15, 2026

The short version CyberReady is fully anonymous — no account, no email, no name required. Your learning data stays on your device and is never sent to us. We rely on three third-party services (RevenueCat, OneSignal, Expo) for subscriptions, notifications, and crash/update delivery. We collect no location, camera, microphone, contacts, advertising, or analytics data.

Who we are

CyberReady (the “app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a cybersecurity education and training app published by Kerri Williams. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at tmrw.pod@gmail.com.

This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have. Because CyberReady has no user accounts and no backend database, we hold essentially no personal information about you.

Information stored on your device

The following is created as you use the app and is stored only on your device (in the app’s local AsyncStorage). It never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us — we have no server that receives or stores it:

  • Learning progress — streaks, quiz scores, tools used, and labs completed
  • Bookmarks and flashcard session results
  • Study plan task completion
  • Interview prep cards you’ve reviewed
  • Notification preferences
  • Daily challenge completion state
  • Onboarding answers (your chosen learning path and experience level)
  • App theme preference (light / dark / system)

This data may be included in a device backup that you create through Apple or Google, under their respective privacy policies — not ours.

Information collected by third-party services

CyberReady uses a small number of third-party SDKs. Each collects only what it needs to perform its function and is governed by its own privacy policy:

RevenueCat — subscription management

Manages your CyberReady Pro subscription state. It collects purchase history, a device identifier, and platform receipt data. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

OneSignal — push notifications

Delivers the notifications you opt into. It collects your device push token, notification interaction data, and a OneSignal player ID. See the OneSignal Privacy Policy.

Expo / EAS — crash reporting and updates

Provides crash reporting and over-the-air update delivery, and may collect basic device information and the app version. See the Expo Privacy Policy.

Information we do not collect

  • No account registration — the app is fully anonymous; no email or name is required
  • No location data
  • No camera or microphone access
  • No contacts
  • No advertising identifiers (there is no ad SDK)
  • No analytics platform (no Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar)

Push notifications

Notifications are sent only if you explicitly grant permission. Categories are: Daily Tips, Streak Reminders, and New Content Alerts. You can opt out of any category in the app’s Notification Preferences screen, or revoke notification permission entirely in your device settings.

Children’s privacy

CyberReady is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from users under 13 (consistent with COPPA). If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us at tmrw.pod@gmail.com.

Your data and how to delete it

Because your learning data lives only on your device, you are always in control of it:

  • Clear it in the app via Settings → Reset Progress, or
  • Remove it entirely by uninstalling the app.

There is no server-side user data to request the deletion of. To manage subscription data, use your App Store or Google Play account. For data held by our SDK providers, use the links in the section above.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. Because CyberReady operates no backend and holds no personal data about you on our servers, there is nothing on our side to access or delete — your on-device data is yours to clear at any time. For any personal data held by our SDK providers (RevenueCat, OneSignal, Expo), please exercise those rights directly with them via their policies linked above. This policy is governed by the laws of the State of California, United States.

How this maps to app store disclosures

Our Apple “Privacy Nutrition Label” and Google Play “Data Safety” declarations reflect the following: Purchases (via RevenueCat), Identifiers (a OneSignal device ID, used for notifications), and Usage Data (Expo crash/update telemetry). Every other category is declared as “Data Not Collected.”

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
