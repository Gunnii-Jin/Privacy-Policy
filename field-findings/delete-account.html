Delete your Field Findings account

In the app

Open Field Findings, go to Settings → Delete Account, and confirm. Your account, field journal, saved photos and usage history are deleted.

Without the app

Email tmrw.pod@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Field Findings account". We delete the account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Nothing from your account is kept after deletion. Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat for billing are governed by their own policies. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
