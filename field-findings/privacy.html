Field Findings Privacy Policy

Introduction

Field Findings ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, and who we share it with when you use our mobile application.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Data We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account information: your email address and display name, or the details Apple shares if you use Sign in with Apple.
  • Photos: images you submit for species identification. If you save a finding, its photo is stored with your journal so you can see it later.
  • Sound recordings: audio you record for sound identification or voice notes. Recordings are processed to make your result and are not stored.
  • Field journal entries: species name, scientific name, your notes, tags, collections, wishlist, the date, and optional GPS coordinates for each finding you save.
  • Journal chat: questions you ask about your journal.
  • Usage data: number of identifications per day, used to enforce free-tier limits.
  • Device information: platform (iOS/Android) and app version, used for debugging.

AI Processing

Identification and the AI features (field notes, deep dives, forecasts, journal chat and others) are provided by Google Gemini, reached through OpenRouter. When you use them, we send the photo, recording or text involved, and the location of the finding if you added one, to that service. It is used only to produce your result.

We ask your permission before anything is sent, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings → Data & Privacy. With it off, the rest of the app keeps working.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Identify species and run the features you ask for.
  • Store and display your personal field journal.
  • Enforce subscription limits and manage your account.
  • Send transactional emails (such as password resets).
  • Diagnose errors and improve app stability.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

Data Retention

Your account data, journal entries and saved photos are kept for as long as your account is active. Deleting a finding deletes its photo. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Third-Party Services

Field Findings uses the following services to run:

  • OpenRouter and Google (Gemini): AI identification and AI features, as described above.
  • Open-Meteo: weather for your forecast, using the approximate coordinates you provide.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscription purchases and entitlements.
  • Apple / Google: payment processing through their app stores, and Sign in with Apple.
  • Vercel and Neon: host our servers and database.
  • Resend: sends password-reset emails.

Each handles data only to provide its service to us.

Children

Field Findings is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: your field journal is always visible in the app.
  • Export your data: use Settings → Export My Data to download a copy of your findings.
  • Delete your data: use Settings → Delete Account to permanently remove your account and all associated data.
  • Correct your data: edit field notes directly in the app.
  • Withdraw AI consent: use Settings → Data & Privacy.

For any other privacy requests, contact us at the address below.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account, field journal, saved photos and usage history.

If you no longer have the app, email tmrw.pod@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Field Findings account". We will delete the account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by email. Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat for billing are governed by their own policies.
