Privacy Policy — Budding

Last updated: August 21, 2026

The short version Budding is a gentle wellness and planting companion. To sync your entries across your devices and household, Budding uses an account and a secure backend. We collect only what the app needs, we never sell your data (including your wellness data), and you can delete everything at any time from Settings. Plant-diagnosis photos are sent to a third-party AI service to generate a result.

Who we are

Budding (the app, we, us, or our) is a gentle wellness and planting app — meal plans, movement, planting and plant diagnosis, recipes, mood check-ins, and gratitude — published by Kerri Williams. For any privacy question or request, contact us at tmrw.pod@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your name and email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address you authorize them to share (with Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email, in which case Apple provides a private relay address). We use this to create and secure your account and to sync your data across devices.

Content you create

We store the content you add so it syncs and stays available to you (and to household members you invite): mood check-ins, gratitude entries and other wellness entries, meal plans, notes, plant photos, planting and journal data, task completion, and household information.

Plant-diagnosis photos

When you submit a photo for plant diagnosis, that image is sent to our third-party AI provider to generate a result (see AI features below).

Subscription information

Budding subscriptions are managed by RevenueCat, which collects purchase history, a device identifier, and platform receipt data to keep your subscription status in sync.

Device and diagnostic information

We may collect device identifiers, app version, and diagnostic or crash information to keep the app stable and secure.

Why we collect it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the app and its features — account creation, syncing, meal plans, wellness tracking, and plant diagnosis (legal basis: performance of our contract with you).
  • To process subscriptions (performance of a contract).
  • To generate AI plant diagnoses when you submit a photo (performance of a contract, and your consent in submitting the photo).
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems (our legitimate interests).
  • To send notifications you enable (your consent).
  • Sensitive wellness data (mood, gratitude, and wellness entries) is processed only to provide the features you use, on the basis of your consent.

Your wellness data is sensitive — and protected

Mood check-ins, gratitude entries, and other wellness data are personal and sensitive. We do not sell this data, and we do not use it for advertising. It is used only to provide the features you choose to use, and it is protected with the safeguards described below. Budding is not a medical device, and nothing in the app is medical advice.

AI features

Plant-diagnosis photos (and any related text) you submit are sent to our third-party AI provider, OpenRouter, which routes the request to an underlying model provider, solely to generate your result. AI-generated content is for informational purposes only and is not professional advice. We do not use your submissions to train our own models. Data you submit is processed by the AI provider under its own privacy policy to fulfill the request and is not retained by us beyond what is needed to return your result.

How we store and protect your data

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS) and stored on secure cloud infrastructure operated by our hosting provider (Vercel) and managed-database provider (Neon), with access limited to what is necessary to operate the app. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant regulators as required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete the associated personal data from our active systems (within about 30 days, allowing for routine backup rotation), except where we must retain limited records to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that make the app work, and only as needed:

  • RevenueCat — subscription management (privacy policy).
  • OpenRouter — AI plant diagnosis (privacy policy).
  • Vercel (hosting) and Neon (database) — they store and process data on our behalf to run the app.
  • Apple and Google — for sign-in and for in-app purchases through their app stores.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

International users and data transfers

We operate from the United States, and our providers may process your data in the U.S. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, your data may be transferred outside your country; where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and our providers data-transfer commitments).

Your privacy rights

If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Our legal bases are described above. We are a small independent developer and are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer; for any request, contact tmrw.pod@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority.

If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA)

In the past 12 months we may have collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (name, email, device IDs), account information, your user content (including sensitive wellness data and photos), commercial information (subscription status), and device and usage data. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. You have the right to know what we collect, to request deletion, to request correction, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We honor opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC). We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

How to delete your data

You can delete your account and its associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app, which calls our account-deletion endpoint (DELETE /api/account) and removes your account and associated data from our systems. You can also email tmrw.pod@gmail.com to request deletion.

Household sharing

If you use household sharing, the entries and information you share become visible to the household members you invite. You are responsible for who you invite; you can manage or remove members in the app.

Children's privacy

Budding is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13 (consistent with COPPA). If you are in a region that sets a higher age of digital consent (such as 16 in parts of the EU), you must meet that age or have appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact tmrw.pod@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Sign in with Apple / Google

If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only the name and email you authorize (Apple may provide a private relay email). We use this information solely to create and manage your account, and it is covered by this policy.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, we send them only with your permission, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new Last updated date, and where required we will notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email tmrw.pod@gmail.com.
