Privacy Policy — Budding

Last updated: August 21, 2026

The short version Budding is a gentle wellness and planting companion. To sync your entries across your devices and household, Budding uses an account and a secure cloud backend. We collect only what the app needs, we never sell your data (including your wellness data), and when you delete your account it is erased immediately and permanently. Photos you submit (plant, receipt, or pantry) are sent to a third-party AI service to generate results.

Who we are

Budding (the app, we, us, or our) is a gentle wellness and planting app — meal plans, movement, planting and plant diagnosis, recipes, mood check-ins, and gratitude — published by Kerri Williams. For any privacy question or request, contact us at tmrw.pod@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your name and email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address you authorize them to share (with Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email, in which case Apple provides a private relay address). We use this to create and secure your account and to sync your data across devices.

Content you create

We store the content you add so it syncs and stays available to you (and to household members you invite): mood check-ins, gratitude entries and other wellness entries, meal plans, notes, planting and journal data, task completion, and household information.

Photos you submit

Budding includes scanning and diagnosis features, so you can submit photos — for example, plant photos for diagnosis, and receipt or pantry photos for scanning. Photos you choose to keep are stored in your account in access-controlled cloud storage, and are sent to our AI provider to generate results (see AI features below).

Subscription information

Budding subscriptions are managed by RevenueCat, which collects purchase history, a device identifier, and platform receipt data to keep your subscription status in sync.

Notification and device information

If you enable push notifications, our provider collects a device push token and interaction data (see Push notifications). We may also collect device identifiers, app version, and diagnostic or crash information to keep the app stable and secure.

Why we collect it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the app and its features — account creation, syncing, meal plans, wellness tracking, plant diagnosis, and receipt/pantry scanning (legal basis: performance of our contract with you).
  • To process subscriptions (performance of a contract).
  • To run AI features when you submit a photo or request (performance of a contract, and your consent in submitting the content).
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems (our legitimate interests).
  • To send notifications you enable (your consent).
  • Sensitive wellness data (mood, gratitude, and wellness entries) is processed only to provide the features you use, on the basis of your consent.

Your wellness data is sensitive — and protected

Mood check-ins, gratitude entries, and other wellness data are personal and sensitive. We do not sell this data, and we do not use it for advertising. It is used only to provide the features you choose to use, and it is protected with the safeguards described below. Budding is not a medical device, and nothing in the app is medical advice.

AI features

Budding uses AI to power features such as plant diagnosis, receipt scanning, pantry photo scanning, and recipe suggestions. When you use one of these features, the photo or text you submit is sent to our third-party AI provider, OpenRouter, which routes the request to Google's Gemini 2.0 Flash model, solely to generate your result. AI-generated content is for informational purposes only and is not professional advice. We do not use your submissions to train our own models, and the AI provider processes them only to fulfill your request under its own policy. Photos you choose to keep are stored in your account until you delete them or your account.

How we store and protect your data

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS). Budding's backend runs on Cloudflare's serverless platform; your account data and content are stored in a managed Postgres database (Neon), and photos and files you upload are stored in access-controlled cloud storage (Cloudflare R2, using private, signed-URL access). Access is limited to what is necessary to operate the app, and data is encrypted at rest. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant regulators as required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account from Settings → Delete Account, your data is permanently and immediately deleted from our systems in a single operation — there is no grace period and no way to recover it afterward. We may retain limited records only where required to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that make the app work, and only as needed:

  • RevenueCat — subscription management (privacy policy).
  • OpenRouter (routing to Google Gemini) — AI plant diagnosis, receipt/pantry scanning, and recipe suggestions (privacy policy).
  • OneSignal — push notifications (privacy policy).
  • Cloudflare (serverless compute and file storage) and Neon (managed Postgres database) — they store and process data on our behalf to run the app.
  • Apple and Google — for sign-in and for in-app purchases through their app stores.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

International users and data transfers

We operate from the United States, and our providers may process your data in the U.S. and other countries. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, your data may be transferred outside your country; where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and our providers data-transfer commitments).

Your privacy rights

If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Our legal bases are described above. We are a small independent developer and are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer; for any request, contact tmrw.pod@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority.

If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA)

In the past 12 months we may have collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (name, email, device and push identifiers), account information, your user content (including sensitive wellness data and photos), commercial information (subscription status), and device and usage data. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. You have the right to know what we collect, to request deletion, to request correction, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We honor opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC). We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

How to delete your data

You can delete your account and its associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app, which calls our account-deletion endpoint (DELETE /api/account) and permanently and immediately removes your account and associated data from our systems. You can also email tmrw.pod@gmail.com to request deletion.

Household sharing

If you use household sharing, the entries and information you share become visible to the household members you invite. You are responsible for who you invite; you can manage or remove members in the app.

Push notifications

If you enable notifications, Budding delivers them through OneSignal, which collects your device push token, a OneSignal player ID, and notification-interaction data in order to send the reminders and updates you turn on. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your device settings. See the OneSignal Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Budding is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13 (consistent with COPPA). If you are in a region that sets a higher age of digital consent (such as 16 in parts of the EU), you must meet that age or have appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact tmrw.pod@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Sign in with Apple / Google

If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only the name and email you authorize (Apple may provide a private relay email). We use this information solely to create and manage your account, and it is covered by this policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new Last updated date, and where required we will notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email tmrw.pod@gmail.com.
