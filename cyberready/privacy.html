Privacy Policy — CyberReady

Last updated: September 30, 2026

The short version You can use CyberReady as a guest without an account; in that case your learning data stays on your device. If you create an account (email, Apple, or Google), we store your account details and sync your learning progress to our cloud database so it follows you across devices. We use RevenueCat for subscriptions and OneSignal for push notifications. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking SDKs, and we collect no location, camera, microphone, or contacts data.

Who we are

CyberReady (the “app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a cybersecurity education and training app published by Kerri Williams. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at tmrw.pod@gmail.com.

This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

Using CyberReady as a guest

If you choose Continue as Guest, no account is created and your learning data is stored only on your device (in the app’s local storage). It is not sent to our servers. If you later create an account, your guest progress and bookmarks are moved into that account and synced as described below.

Account information

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and a securely hashed password (email sign-up), or the identifier and email address provided by Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In (Apple may provide a private relay address);
  • Name, if you enter it at sign-up or your Apple/Google account provides it;
  • a unique user ID assigned to your account.

We use this to sign you in, keep your account secure, send account emails (such as email verification and password resets), and link your subscription to your account.

Learning data synced to your account

When you are signed in, the following is stored in our cloud database (hosted by Supabase) and cached on your device so the app works offline:

  • Learning progress — points, streaks, quiz and exam scores, tools used, glossary terms and cheat sheets viewed, labs completed, recent activity
  • Bookmarks
  • Onboarding answers and your chosen learning path
  • Pro feature progress — Security+ domain checklist and target exam date, study plan tasks, certification roadmap, SOC simulator high score

Some feature state (for example interview prep cards reviewed, daily challenge results, and theme and notification preferences) is stored only on your device.

We use this data only to provide the app’s features to you. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the service providers below.

Service providers

CyberReady relies on these providers, each acting on our behalf and governed by its own privacy policy:

Supabase — accounts and cloud database

Hosts user accounts, authentication, and the synced learning data described above. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.

Apple and Google — sign-in

If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, that provider authenticates you and shares your account identifier, email address, and (optionally) name with us.

RevenueCat — subscription management

Manages your CyberReady Pro subscription. It receives purchase history, platform receipt data, a device identifier, and, if you are signed in, your CyberReady user ID so your subscription works across devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

OneSignal — push notifications

Delivers the notifications you opt into. It collects your device push token, notification interaction data, and a OneSignal subscription ID. See the OneSignal Privacy Policy.

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never receive your payment card details.

Information we do not collect

  • No location data
  • No camera, microphone, photo library, or contacts access
  • No advertising identifiers or ad SDKs
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites

Push notifications

Notifications are sent only if you explicitly grant permission. Categories are: Daily Tips, Streak Reminders, and New Content Alerts. You can opt out of any category in the app’s Notification Preferences screen, or revoke notification permission entirely in your device settings.

Data retention and deletion

  • Delete your account in the app via Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and the learning data synced to it, and removes that account’s data from your device.
  • Reset your progress without deleting your account via Settings → Reset Progress; bookmarks can be cleared via Settings → Clear Bookmarks.
  • Guest data is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • You can also email tmrw.pod@gmail.com to request deletion.

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; manage that in your App Store or Google Play account.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to synced data is restricted to your own account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children’s privacy

CyberReady is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (consistent with COPPA). If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at tmrw.pod@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to processing. To exercise these rights, use the in-app options above or email tmrw.pod@gmail.com. We process account and learning data to provide the service you requested (performance of a contract). We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. This policy is governed by the laws of the State of California, United States.

How this maps to app store disclosures

Our Apple “App Privacy” and Google Play “Data Safety” declarations reflect: Contact Info (email address, name), Identifiers (user ID; OneSignal device ID), Purchases (via RevenueCat), and Usage Data / User Content (learning progress and bookmarks) — all used for app functionality, linked to your account when you are signed in, and not used for tracking.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
