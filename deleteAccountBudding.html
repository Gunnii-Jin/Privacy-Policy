Delete your account — Budding

Budding, published by Kerri Williams

The short version Delete your account in the app from Settings → Delete Account, or email tmrw.pod@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Your account and its data are deleted permanently.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Budding and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately, and you're signed out.

Delete it without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email tmrw.pod@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Budding account. Send it from the email address on your account (if you used Sign in with Apple with a hidden email, tell us the name on the account and roughly when you signed up). We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email, and sign-in details.
  • Everything you've added: mood check-ins, gratitude and journal entries, habits, movement logs, meal plans, recipes, plants and garden records, pantry, notes, and settings.
  • Plant photos stored for your account.

Deletion is permanent. There's no grace period and the data can't be recovered.

What stays

  • If you share a household, the shared lists you added to (pantry, shopping, tasks, calendar) stay with the other members, without your name or account attached.
  • Limited records we're required to keep for legal, tax, or accounting reasons.
  • Subscriptions aren't cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel Budding Premium in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings first, so you aren't charged again.

More detail is in the Budding Privacy Policy.
