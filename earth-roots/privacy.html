Earth Roots Privacy Policy

Introduction

Earth Roots ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, and who we share it with when you use our mobile application.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Data We Collect

Earth Roots does not require an account, and we do not run servers that store your personal information. The app keeps the following on your device only:

  • Your progress: eco-actions and habits you track, points earned, and your streak.
  • App preferences, such as settings you change in the app.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Earth Roots Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information (platform, app version). It does not receive your name or email from us.

Images and Links

Some pictures in the app load from Unsplash, which sees your device's IP address when an image downloads, as with any website. When you tap a link to a charity or another website, it opens outside Earth Roots and that site's own privacy policy applies.

How We Use Your Data

  • To show your progress in the app.
  • To unlock Earth Roots Pro when you have an active subscription.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat: manages subscription purchases and entitlements.
  • Apple / Google: payment processing through their app stores.
  • Unsplash: hosts some images shown in the app.

Each handles data only to provide its service.

Children

Earth Roots is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights and Deleting Your Data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you control it completely: deleting the app deletes it. There is no account to close. Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat for billing are governed by their own policies. For any other privacy request, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

As we add features (for example community or sharing features), this policy will be updated before those features go live, and the date above will change.
